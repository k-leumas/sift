Build the repeatable spike probe (CONTEXT, Claude's discretion: scripted, in-repo, rerunnable after Bridge upgrades). It is one CLI command that measures what SPK-01..04 and D-43 ask:
- capabilities and CONDSTORE/QRESYNC behaviour
- label folders and the apply/remove mechanics
- Message-ID and internal-ID consistency and duplicates

It reports aggregates only, and it changes the mailbox only in the owner-approved, bounded label test.

Purpose: the live spike (02-14) runs this against the owner's real Proton mailbox. The command must be safe and private by construction, and proven against a real IMAP server first.
