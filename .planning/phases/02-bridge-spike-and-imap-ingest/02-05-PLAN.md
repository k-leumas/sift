<objective>
Give the scheduler the two hooks Phase 2 ingest needs:
- `nudge(mailboxId)`: a mailbox becomes due now, without breaking "never overlaps itself" (D-28). Nothing calls it in Phase 2; a future IDLE listener or sync command will.
- An `AbortSignal` passed to `runBatch`: graceful shutdown stops a chunked ingest between chunks (D-04, P1 D-53).

Purpose: both change the supervisor's contract, so they land early and independently, ahead of the worker integration (02-13) that consumes the signal.

Output: supervisor changes and fake-timer tests.
</objective>
