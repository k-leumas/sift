<objective>
Close verification gap 3 (review CR-03, a regression of Phase 2's fail-closed volume valve, D-26): spend the owner's one-time resume approval with the ingest that used it, inside the ingest lock, instead of after the classification pass.

Since 03-09, with classification on, runBatch skips the in-lock recordSyncSuccess and clears the valve counts only in the post-pass status write for done, busy, aborted, rules_invalid and infra. If the pass throws anything else (a pg error, the MAX_CONSECUTIVE_MESSAGE_FAILURES rethrow, a failed status write) or the process dies mid-pass, approved_new_count stays set, the state becomes error, the next batch passes the hold check and ingests with approved + cap, and that repeats until a batch succeeds.

Purpose: the valve stays fail-closed whatever classification does.
Output: status.ts recordIngestSynced, its in-lock call in mailbox-batch.ts, unit tests and a batch-level resume-then-throw test.
</objective>
