Add the owner-facing connection and ingest settings decided in D-73/D-74 to the strict config schema:
- `imap.tls.mode` and `imap.tls.pin_sha256` per mailbox

Output: the extended `schema.ts`, its re-exports, the example config and the config tests.
</objective>
