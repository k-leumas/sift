<objective>
Make the apply executor robust: count failed attempts per action with fixed reason codes, give up after LABEL_ACTION_MAX_ATTEMPTS, resolve unconfirmed COPYs by searching the label folder before any retry, stop cleanly on a dropped connection, and prove (with a fake that behaves like Bridge's gluon) that Sift never duplicates a copy or claims the owner's, and that every write it made has its label_event row.

Purpose: D-17 (failed COPY handling), the crash-safety of Research Pattern 3, and ACT-03's "every change is recorded" under failure, before the real Bridge sees any of it in the live run.
Output: failure handling in apply.ts and run.ts, a gluon-like fake LabelSession and a failure test file.
</objective>
