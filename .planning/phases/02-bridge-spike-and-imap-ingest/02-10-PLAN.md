<objective>
Implement the sync engine as pure orchestration over the `FolderSource` and `IngestStore` contracts. It covers:
- first sync with the default 30-day backfill, which runs throttled and uncapped with progress (D-74, D-75)
- polling new mail with the INTERNALDATE gate and the n:* filter (D-18..D-21)
- chunked, resumable commits (D-04)
- the volume valve for polling and resyncs (D-26)
- removal tracking (D-17)
- the generation-based UIDVALIDITY resync (D-22..D-25)
- the count-then-run CLI backfill entry points (D-75)

Purpose: this is where duplicates, missed mail and accidental reclassification of history would come from. Keeping it free of I/O lets every rule be tested exhaustively with in-memory fakes. The adapter (02-09) and the database store (02-13) are then thin.

Output: `apps/worker/src/ingest/{plan,run}.ts`, in-memory fakes and the engine and resync test suites.
</objective>
