<objective>
Give the owner the three commands the ingest design promises:
- `sift mailbox resume <slug>` releases a volume hold (D-26)
- `sift mailbox list` shows the new states, first-backfill progress and counts (D-75; Claude's discretion: ingest observability)
- `sift mailbox backfill <slug> --days N` is the explicit later backfill: it shows the count, asks for confirmation, then runs uncapped (D-75) and serialised against the worker (D-03)

Purpose: the volume valve fails closed and only the owner can reopen it. The backfill is how the owner pulls in older mail on demand, always seeing the count before anything is ingested.

Output: three command modules, the owner-side registry functions, and tests.
</objective>
