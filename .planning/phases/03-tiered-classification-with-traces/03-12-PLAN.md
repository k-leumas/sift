Give the owner the explicit re-run D-17 describes: `sift classify <slug>` for one message, a date range or everything pending, with count and confirmation, under the mailbox lock, appending new decisions and refetching expired bodies from IMAP by identity (D-32).

Purpose: decisions are never redone automatically (D-15); this is the one door to redo them, for example after editing rules.yaml or pulling a new model.
Output: the classify command, the refetch module, command registration and tests.
