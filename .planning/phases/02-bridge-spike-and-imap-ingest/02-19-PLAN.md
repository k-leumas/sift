## Flagged assumptions

- Bridge may never change UIDVALIDITY on restart or repair (the spike measures it). Then the simulated mismatch is the only way to force the resync path on real Proton identities: it proves the engine resyncs real mail without duplicates or eligibility changes, but not that Bridge itself triggers it. The record names the method used.
- A busy real inbox receives mail during the run. The restart and resync checks therefore compare rows by UID and creation time, not total counts.
- The criterion 4 timing starts when the light probe first sees the new UID (10 s granularity), so 20 s of slack is allowed on top of the poll interval.
