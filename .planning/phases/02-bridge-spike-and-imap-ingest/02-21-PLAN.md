  apps/worker/src/runtime/mailbox-batch.ts   runBatch calls recordMailboxSeen inside the ingest lock BEFORE connecting; onBatchError maps unreachable/timeout inside STARTUP_GRACE_MS (60 s) to connecting, anything else or later to error; password_missing and not_in_config are errors.
