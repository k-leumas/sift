<objective>
Implement the owner-side mailbox lifecycle (FND-02, D-32..D-34, D-63, D-69): `sift config apply` with its safety guards, `sift mailbox rename`, `sift mailbox list`, and a pure registry diff that the worker's drift check reuses.

Purpose: config.yaml is the source of truth, and the registry is its reconciled copy in Postgres. Safe changes apply automatically. Risky ones (a disappearing slug next to a new one) stop and ask, so a typo never orphans a mailbox's history under a new id.

Output: `@sift/db/registry-plan`, `@sift/db/registry`, three CLI command modules, library and CLI tests.
</objective>
