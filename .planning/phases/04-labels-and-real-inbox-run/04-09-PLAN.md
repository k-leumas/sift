<objective>
Install the apply executor in the label step: on an apply-mode mailbox, carry out each planned step through the 04-02 gate, recording intent before every write and the outcome after it. Covers labelling (with CREATE on first use), holds, keeps, moves and review removals, the "already present" presence check, skips for mail that left the folder, the case rule and conflicts, and the resync guard.

Purpose: this is ROADMAP success criterion 1 and 2 (a confident message shows up under its label; a held one gets none, with the reason recorded) and criterion 4 (every change in label_event, every label marked Sift-applied). Failure accounting (attempts, failed, unconfirmed copies, gluon semantics) follows in 04-10.
Output: labels/apply.ts, labels/folders.ts, the apply arm in run.ts and two test files.
</objective>

|----------|-------------|
| worker -> owner's mailbox | Real label writes on the owner's Proton account |
| owner's existing labels -> Sift's records | Copies Sift did not make must never be recorded or removed as Sift's |
